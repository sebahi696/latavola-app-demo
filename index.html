Try the full flow

  1. In the phone: add items (choose the required options), open Cart → Checkout, pick Pickup, enter a name and phone, Place order.
  2. Open the operator panel below in a new tab (restaurant latavola, any email, password demo), tap Start shift — the new order rings. Open it, accept with an ETA, move it to Preparing → Ready → Delivered.
  3. Back in the phone the order status updates live; after delivery the points are earned.

Loyalty and delivery

Sign in from Profile or Rewards with any phone number — the demo OTP code is 123456. Points: 1 per $1, worth $0.07 each, usable at checkout (max 50% of the order). Coupon WELCOME10 gives 10% off.

Delivery: add an address from Checkout (pick an area such as Hamra, Verdun, Salim Salam) and the fee is applied. The live app uses GPS to find the area automatically.

What the real version adds

Native Android/iOS app from the stores · push notifications on every status change · WhatsApp OTP · the operator panel installed on the cashier's phone with ring + notifications · real delivery areas from the QR Menu · open/closed, busy mode, areas on/off, daily report, staff accounts.
